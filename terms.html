Program and consent

The ReSwap account messaging program sends verification codes that you request and, only if you separately opt in, optional account and group or TripShare invitation notices. This program does not send marketing messages or unsolicited direct messages.

Choosing Send verification code in ReSwap requests a verification text to the number you enter; choosing Resend requests another code. A verification request does not enroll you in optional notices. Optional SMS notices use a separate choice that is off by default. You must affirmatively enable that choice to receive them. An invitation sent by another member, a stored phone number, or accepting the general Terms of Service is not SMS consent.

SMS consent is not a condition of purchase or general app access. You can use ReSwap without subscribing to optional SMS notices. Certain phone-based features require verification that you own the number.

Frequency and costs

Message frequency varies with your requests and account activity. Message and data rates may apply. ReSwap does not charge a separate SMS subscription fee; your wireless provider's charges may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt out and support

Reply STOP to a ReSwap text to opt out of the SMS program. You will receive an opt-out confirmation and no further program messages unless you opt back in. You may also turn off optional SMS notices in ReSwap. Reply START to resume SMS delivery after an SMS opt-out; optional notices still require your separate in-app SMS preference to be enabled.

Reply HELP for help or email support@reswap.org. If a requested code cannot be delivered, contact support rather than sharing a code or using a phone number you do not own.

Your information

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for promotional or marketing purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for marketing or unrelated purposes. See the ReSwap Privacy Policy for the limited processing needed to deliver messages and protect the service.
