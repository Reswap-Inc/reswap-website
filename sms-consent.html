ReSwap SMS consent and verification

Native-app consent information, October 17, 2026.

This page explains the SMS choices shown in the ReSwap mobile app. It is not an SMS signup form: this website does not collect phone numbers or enroll visitors in text messages.

Requested verification codes

On the app's phone verification screen, a user enters the phone number they own and taps Send verification code to explicitly request a verification text. The SMS disclosure is shown before that button. There is no verification-code consent checkbox: the deliberate code-request button is the action. A code request authorizes the requested verification text, not future account or invitation notices. Resending requests another code.

Optional account and invitation notices

The app presents a separate, optional SMS choice for account and group or TripShare invitation notices. This choice is off by default. The user must enable it to record their consent. It is separate from accepting the general Terms of Service and is not required to request a verification code, make a purchase, or use the app generally.

Another member entering someone's phone number, importing a contact, or creating an invitation does not authorize an SMS. Optional invitation texts are limited to recipients with verified phone ownership and their own recorded SMS consent. Other invitations stay in the app or use eligible email delivery; this is not an unsolicited direct-message service.

Disclosures and preferences

The app identifies ReSwap and the purpose of the messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help, or contact support@reswap.org. Optional SMS consent is not a condition of purchase or general app access. No mobile information is sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for promotional or marketing purposes.

Users can turn off optional notices in the app. SMS opt-out is also honored at the messaging provider. Resuming provider delivery does not create the user's separate optional account or invitation consent.

The disclosure links directly to the ReSwap Privacy Policy and ReSwap SMS Terms, both publicly accessible without signing in.
